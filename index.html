About Melisa Supermarket

Welcome to Melisa Supermarket, your trusted local supermarket in Zambia for over 28 years. We offer a wide range of fresh groceries, household essentials, and personal care products at affordable prices.

Our shelves are stocked with fresh fruits and vegetables, meats, dairy products, baked goods, household items, and personal care products — making your shopping convenient and enjoyable.

With three convenient locations across Lusaka, we're committed to serving our community with quality products and exceptional customer service.
